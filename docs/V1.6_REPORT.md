# v1.6.0 — Physical World & Visual Polish Update

Required implementation and packaged acceptance passed. Private release publication is the remaining final gate. Original specification: [V1.6_REQUIREMENTS.txt](V1.6_REQUIREMENTS.txt).

## 1. Goal
Make existing use, depletion, power, occupancy, damage and control visible in the world. Preserve v1.5 authoritative behavior rather than adding scripted interactions.

## 2. Existing visual baseline
v1.5 mixed village/ruin geometry with generic facility boxes. Facilities relied on labels, hazards used translucent cylinders, robots bobbed as whole models, death shrank bodies, and focus snapped. Baseline captured Seed 7, exactly t=90, viewport 1920×1080, camera (37,23,43), target (0,3,0): `images/v1.5-baseline-seed7-t90.png`.

## 3. Art direction
Stylized low-poly observatory: muted concrete, wood and industrial metal, cool medical accents, limited warm flame/lamplight. No external art packs, raster textures, downloaded sound files or CJK font binaries. Controlled and contested states use physical rings/flags and shape/pulse as well as identity color.

## 4. Blender pipeline
`Art/Blender/generate_world_assets.py` runs with Blender CLI/bpy; editable `physical_world_kit.blend` is retained. Nineteen named pieces export to `public/assets/world-kit.glb` (27,856 bytes). Ten material roles, no texture dependencies. `npm run assets` rebuilds both robots and the kit; set BLENDER_PATH if Blender is elsewhere. The GLB is loaded once with a procedural fallback, and normalized pieces are assembled/merged at runtime. It is small enough that Draco/Meshopt dependencies are unnecessary. Original robot GLBs and sources remain unchanged.

## 5. POI redesign
Village uses raised open houses, pitched roofs, windows, common paving, fences and lamps. Clinic uses white treatment paving, cabinets, beds and an illuminated medical cross. Watchtower has elevated deck, columns, ladder rungs, rails and beacon; existing occupants render on its platform. Ruins have broken columns, walls and debris. Depot has racks, crates, metal canopy and loading apron. Shelter has warm canopy, benches, beds and fire. Northern bridge has an actual deck, supports and rails aligned with the existing flood position. Lake has bounded water, stones, reeds, shore path and a collection dock. Paths connect original coordinates, with no movement cost changes. Vegetation and rocks are cleared around landmarks for readability.

## 6. Interactable visual states
Doors use edge hinges and visible lock/damage indicators. Cache lids and contents distinguish full/partial/empty/searched. Generator shows stationary/offline, damaged casing/sparks, repair progress/sparks and online rotating motor/light. Medical indicators distinguish unpowered, active, occupied and depleted. Firepit logs remain visible when unlit/extinguished; flame size and light distinguish lit/low fuel. These projections read existing snapshot state and never invent resources, charges or gameplay states.

## 7. Agent animation
Named GLB limbs receive procedural gait, body bob, eased visual velocity, idle sway, contextual facing, search bend/arms, repair strokes, treatment/rest pose and radio gestures. Attacks have anticipation, impact emission and hit reaction; flight leans forward. Death eases into full-size shutdown with dark optics, leaving an inert body throughout the match. Render interpolation does not move authoritative agents. Animation uses simulation time and stable identifier phases, with wall-clock delta only for interpolation.

## 8. Environment
Open-sided courtyards and cut-back canopies make clinic, shelter and depot observable and usable from existing approach directions. No opaque new building shell blocks a v1.5 route; existing authoritative walls and door portals remain unchanged. This deliberately avoids introducing a new navigation/collision system. It is a semi-interior art solution, not a complete indoor navmesh.

## 9. Weather / day-night
Rain uses bounded line streaks; storm increases streak speed, tilt, opacity, foliage sway and fog, with lower sun intensity. Wet facilities use lower roughness. Twilight shifts sun color; night has moon/star shader accents, cool lighting and powered lamps/windows/interior fixtures. Clinic/village powered emissions and equipment respond to the generator; shelter is independently lit. Lake shader shimmer and ocean movement remain inexpensive. No fluid simulation, physical reflections or volumetric atmosphere.

## 10. Damage / history traces
`visualHistory` derives marks from existing ATTACK/BETRAYAL/FIRE_STARTED/BRIDGE_BLOCKED/POI_CONTROLLED/POI_RELEASED/GENERATOR_REPAIRED/CONTAINER_SEARCHED events. Combat/search marks last 60 sim seconds, control traces 90; major fire/repair traces survive to the match end, within a 96-mark budget. Affected POIs gain ground overlays and debris (clean/used/damaged projection). Live reload seeds prior history from the existing export; replay rebuilds from complete historical events at the displayed time. No visual traces are added to authoritative saves.

## 11. Cinematic mode
Observatory retains Inspector, Feed and Director. Cinematic expands the world to the available stage, hides the lower dashboard/Inspector and shows Alive/time/weather/focused Agent plus major-event banner. Playback and exit controls remain available. Major events use the existing localization/event formatter. Dialogue is a separate bounded overlay (at most three, six sim-second TTL, selected Agent first); user/model prose remains escaped text and is not forcibly translated. Close-range interaction status uses existing bilingual action keys.

## 12. Camera
Focus interpolates target/position; follow retains relative camera offset. POI labels and physical geometry can focus a place. Event focus prioritizes winner/death/betrayal/fire/control/repair/combat, with ten-second cooldown, fresh-event window and twelve-second manual-camera suppression. Manual zoom cancels a pending focus. Near labels omit POI names; medium shows names/state; far shows short POI labels and Agent clusters. An explicit labels toggle supports inspecting the physical art.

## 13. Replay
Rendering uses frame.world, frame agents and events at or before the displayed time. It never substitutes the final door/generator/hazard/control state. Full visual event history is a frontend projection only. Backward seeks clear accumulated traces; missing legacy world data hides the physical layer. v1.5 saves and v1.4/v1.5 Stories require no migration. Existing sampled replay resolution remains five sim seconds; unsampled brief interactions cannot be reconstructed as exact animation footage.

## 14. Performance

Final performance pass groups each robot into body/head/left-right arm/leg assemblies. Source material colors are baked into vertices, while emissive optics remain separate; named pivots preserve contextual gestures and locomotion. Original GLB files stay unchanged. This reduces approximately 23 source meshes per robot to eight draws, including the head/limb surfaces, rather than adding a skeletal rig. The extra assembly test verifies reduced mesh count, independent pivots and retained vertex identity colors.
Static kit geometry merges by palette per landmark. Repeated trees/rocks use instancing and shared wind shader uniforms. No textures in the kit, no skinned animation, no per-agent LLM work in rendering. The renderer counter sums the complete composed frame, including shadows and postprocessing; it must not be mistaken for the final fullscreen OutputPass alone. Texture count includes renderer-created bloom/shadow render targets, not downloaded image textures. Final Browser/Electron samples and counts are recorded under docs/qa; sample duration is about two seconds and hardware/load dependent. No fixed hardware FPS guarantee.

Measured on RTX 3070 Ti Laptop GPU, ANGLE/D3D11, 1920×1080: ordinary Browser 89.3 FPS at 1× / 71.6 at 10×; actual Electron 62.1 / 51.2 while other QA was active. Storm/fire stress samples are recorded in physical-browser-v1.6.json. Samples are approximately two seconds, not a long-running frame-time guarantee. Day scene is 599 composed-frame calls / 108,823 triangles versus 875 before robot batching, with identical triangle count. Kit GLB is 27,856 bytes, 19 meshes, eight exported materials and zero image textures. Renderer estimates about 68.2 MiB for 14 textures including render targets; this is an estimate, not GPU memory telemetry. Shared geometry and visual label tiers address the current budget; no additional geometry LOD is claimed.

## 15. Determinism
No changes to core/, server/ or config/ gameplay code. v1.5's 50 primary + 50 holdout golden is executed without --update and remains byte-equivalent in its expected facts/hash results. Visual math never calls the simulation RNG. New tests verify snapshot/export and RNG state preservation, state projections, actual hinge/lid/light/motor transforms, limb poses, impact emission, shutdown and history TTL/backward filtering. Visual-only island RNG is independently constructed as before.

## 16. QA
Passed: 34 unit/integration/visual tests and the unchanged 50 primary + 50 holdout v1.5 golden. Required suites: npm test, test:benchmark, test:i18n, test:ui, test:results, test:story, test:desktop, test:story:desktop, world Browser/Electron, visual scenarios, portable and payload integrity. Visual scenarios cover all twenty requested states, plus backward-seek replay and storm/fire 1x/10x progression. Desktop viewports 1920×1080 and 1366×768; Story includes 390×844. QA fixtures intentionally place characters/set facility states to inspect rendering; they are test-only and do not affect normal deterministic runs. Browser plugin not available; Playwright Chrome and actual packaged Electron are used. Initial shader-newline and bubble/button overflow defects were caught and fixed through real rendering QA.

## 17. Before / after
`images/v1.5-baseline-seed7-t90.png` and `images/v1.6-baseline-seed7-t90.png` use matching Seed/time/camera/viewport. Eight required named screenshots show actual renderer output. State fixture images are identified as acceptance scenarios rather than evidence that a scripted seed will naturally produce every interaction. Original screenshots and historical release documentation remain available.

## 18. Known limitations
Semi-interiors intentionally retain open arcades/cutaway canopies to respect v1.5 traversal; decorative props have no new collision gameplay. Rigless limb motion is lightweight and approximate. Marks are bounded event projections, not a physical destruction simulation. Fire is confined to the authoritative hazard radius and does not spread; flood visualization corresponds to the local northern bridge blocker, not island-wide flooding. Existing simple procedural audio now distinguishes world events; positional audio is optional/not implemented. Story event image archiving and demo MP4 are P1/non-P0 and not release gates. No full indoor navmesh, fluid, bridge repair gameplay, barricade construction, permanent footprint map or trailer generation is claimed.
