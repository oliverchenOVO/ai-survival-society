# v1.6 — Physical World & Visual Polish

## Baseline audit
The v1.5 scene mixes original village/ruin art with generic boxes for facilities. Clinic, depot and shelter are primarily recognizable through floating labels. Doors rotate about their centre; caches have no lid or contents; equipment indicators do not explain power, charge or repair. Robot GLBs have individually named limbs, but the renderer currently only bobs the complete model. Death scales a robot to 45%, obscuring the history. Hazards are translucent cylinders. Camera focus snaps; cinematic currently only enables event focus, without changing the dashboard.

## POI and Blender strategy
Use a limited concrete/wood/metal/medical/industrial/glass/water/vegetation/emission palette. Generate an editable modular Blender kit and lightweight GLB with named wall, roof, door, fence, crate, shelf, bed, lamp, generator, radio, cabinet, platform, bridge, debris, barricade, sign, pipe and cable pieces. Assemble eight recognizable landmarks around the existing authoritative coordinates. Clinic, shelter and depot use open-sided treatment/storage courtyards and overhead canopies: no added collision or closed decorative wall across an existing route. Retire duplicated original building art where replaced.

## Runtime animation and history
Pure visual-state derivation reads snapshots and existing Event Bus entries. It never writes to snapshots or consumes simulation RNG. Hinged lids/doors, motor rotation, equipment lights, flame/smoke, bounded particles, contextual robot limbs and persistent shutdown wrecks use elapsed simulation time plus stable hash phases. Derive bounded visual traces from events at or before the displayed replay time; rebuild on backward seeks. No authoritative save fields or Story semantics change.

## Performance budget
Merge static kit geometry by material; instance vegetation and rocks; cap hazard particles, dialogue and trace counts. Share geometries/materials. Limit point lights and use emissive fixtures for remaining lights. Record renderer calls/triangles/geometries/textures and asset bytes alongside RAF samples for 12 agents at 1x/10x and storm/fire. Target near 60 FPS in ordinary 1920×1080 scenes; publish measured hardware-dependent results rather than promises.

## Camera, UI and replay
Smooth focus/follow and POI picking, prioritized event focus with cooldown and user interaction suppression. Observatory retains the dashboard; cinematic expands the world and uses minimal localized HUD/banner. Zoom-aware labels and bounded dialogue/status bubbles reduce clutter. Replay uses frame.world and frame agents, never the final equipment state. Missing old world/event data safely yields baseline art and no invented traces.
